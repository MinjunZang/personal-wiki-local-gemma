# DTCS Product Overview

DTCS connects various data types, including computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, and experimental data, to provide faster chemical understanding. This capability helps interpret spectra and infer reaction mechanisms, which can reduce experimental iteration and save on characterization resources. The product aims to guide experimental design by suggesting the next experiment.

## Key points
- DTCS connects computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, experimental data, and AI optimization to help interpret spectra and infer reaction mechanisms. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 1: DTCS*
- Faster interpretation and mechanistic insight can reduce experimental iteration, lab time, and use of scarce characterization resources. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 1: DTCS*
- The value proposition is to turn experimental spectra into faster chemical understanding. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- DTCS can simulate/predict spectra and infer reaction mechanisms. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
