# Target Customer Segments

The three primary customer groups identified are Big Tech, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors, each facing distinct pain points in chemical R&D. These pain points revolve around long experimental cycles, high costs, and the need for faster data interpretation. The goal is to validate the severity of these pain points through customer interviews.

## Key points
- Big Tech/Established R&D groups include materials & design engineers working with plastic, coatings, adhesives, and other reaction-driven materials. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- Pain points for Big Tech include long experiment/characterization cycles, high cost of lab time & instrumentation, and a need for stronger theory ↔ experiment feedback. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- Resource-Constrained Startups have limited people, budget, and characterization capacity, and need to move from data to decision faster. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- Raw-Material Vendors' pain points include needing evidence behind material performance and that customer-specific development can be slow. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- The initial hypothesis suggests that the pain may be strongest where experiments and characterization are expensive. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- The potential value proposition is turning experimental spectra into faster chemical understanding by connecting computational chemistry with real-time experimental data. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
