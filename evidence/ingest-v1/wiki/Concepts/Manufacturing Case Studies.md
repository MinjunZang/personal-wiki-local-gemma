# Manufacturing Case Studies

This note analyzes how digital twin technology can bridge the gap between virtual simulation and physical manufacturing processes across industries like consumer electronics and semiconductors. The core value lies in using simulation to pre-screen optimal solutions, thereby saving time and reducing costly physical trial-and-error. Ultimately, the system's credibility requires continuous calibration against real-world experimental data.
