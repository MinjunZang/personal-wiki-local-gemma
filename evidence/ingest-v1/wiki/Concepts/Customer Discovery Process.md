# Customer Discovery Process

The customer discovery process involves validating hypotheses through interviews focused on pain severity, current workflow, and willingness to pay. The methodology requires understanding the customer's existing process before presenting the product. Key segments include Big Tech R&D, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors.
