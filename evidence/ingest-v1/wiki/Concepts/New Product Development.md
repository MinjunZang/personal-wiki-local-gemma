# New Product Development

Digital twins show potential value during New Product Development (NPD) because this phase involves high resource, labor, and trial-and-error costs. Using digital twins can help screen out suboptimal plans early on, thereby saving time and reducing the cost of failure. This capability is particularly valuable when simulating complex processes before physical testing.

## Key points
- 系统在新产品开发阶段（NPD）尤其可能有价值，因为这一阶段资源、人力和试错成本都很高，数字孪生有助于提前筛选方案。 — *[[Digital Twin Manufacturing Applications]], 时间线与实践安排*
- 如果系统能先在虚拟环境中对多种因素进行模拟并排序，就能先筛出更优方案，再进入真实试产，从而节省时间、减少试错成本。 — *[[Digital Twin Manufacturing Applications]], 互动与案例分析 + 互动与案例分析 > 典型案例*
