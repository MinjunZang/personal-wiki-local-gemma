# Chemical R&D Pain Points

Core challenges in chemical research include slow characterization cycles and the need for stronger feedback loops between theory and experiment. Pain points are observed across different customer segments, such as Big Tech R&D and resource-constrained startups. The goal is to improve interpretation speed and guide the next experiment to reduce resource use.

- Chemical characterization is slowed by weak real-time theory $\leftrightarrow$ experiment feedback — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 4: Iteration Reflection*
- Pain-point hypothesis includes long experiment / characterization cycles — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- There is a need for stronger theory $\leftrightarrow$ experiment feedback — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- The value proposition is to turn experimental spectra into faster chemical understanding. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- DTCS connects computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, experimental data, and AI optimization to help interpret spectra and infer reaction mechanisms. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 1: DTCS*
- Faster interpretation and mechanistic insight can reduce experimental iteration, lab time, and use of scarce characterization resources. — *[[Digital Twin Chemical Science]], Slide 1: DTCS*
