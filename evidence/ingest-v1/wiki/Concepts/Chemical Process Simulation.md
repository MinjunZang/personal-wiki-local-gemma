# Chemical Process Simulation

The technology's current focus is on simulating chemical processes, including areas like surface treatment and electrochemistry. This capability allows for establishing links between chemical reactions and observable properties, such as color. The goal is to connect virtual models with physical manufacturing processes to improve prediction and reduce real-world trial and error costs.
