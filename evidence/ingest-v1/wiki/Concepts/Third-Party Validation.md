# Third-Party Validation

Building trust in new technologies requires independent verification to prove reliability and lifespan. Following industry precedents, relying on third-party reports from laboratories and engineers is necessary to build confidence. This validation process helps overcome adoption barriers when competing against established solutions.

## Key points
- Following the solar and battery industry precedent: rely on third-party independent laboratory and engineer verification reports (covering reliability, temperature, and lifespan tests) to build trust. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], Commercialization (¶23–29)*
- Competing against established solutions (e.g., solar + battery) requires third-party validated data rather than just concept marketing. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], 2. Key Risks Identified (¶35–42)*
- Engage Third-Party Auditors: Bring in independent engineering firms/labs to test prototypes and issue validation reports. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], 3. Actionable Next Steps (GTM Strategy) (¶43–47)*
