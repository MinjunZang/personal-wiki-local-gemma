# Early Adopter Markets

Initial market focus should target early adopters in specific sectors like remote power, industrial process heat, and government-supported pilots. These segments are noted as being more willing to participate in innovative or experimental technologies. Targeting these areas allows for smaller-scale validation before tackling larger, more established markets.

## Key points
- Short-Term / Early Adopters include remote and transportable power, industrial process heat and power, and government-supported pilots, universities, local public utilities, and industrial partners. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], Potential Market (¶14–22)*
- Starting with smaller-scale, more flexible solutions like portable, remote, or temporary power is recommended over large-scale MW deployment initially. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], Interviewer / Project: General Alpha (Finance Advisor) + Topic: Customer Interview & Go-To-Market Strategy + 1. Interview Findings — Questions Confirmed + Product / Market (¶1–13)*

- Small-scale pilot projects partnered with state governments, universities, public entities, or corporate R&D/innovation teams can reduce customer adoption risk. — *[[General Alpha Finance Advisor Interview]], Commercialization (¶23–29)*
