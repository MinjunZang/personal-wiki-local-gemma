# Portable Power Solutions

The technology is best suited for smaller-scale, portable, or remote power applications rather than immediate large-scale MW deployments. Early focus should target early adopters in areas like remote and transportable power, while avoiding large-scale deployments in critical infrastructure initially. Success requires demonstrating value through small-scale pilots and third-party validation.
