## Concepts
*Ideas, methods, and market topics*

- [[Chemical Process Simulation]] — The technology's current focus is on simulating chemical processes, including areas like surface treatment and electrochemistry.
- [[Chemical R&D Pain Points]] — Core challenges in chemical research include slow characterization cycles and the need for stronger feedback loops between theory and experiment.
- [[Customer Discovery Process]] — The customer discovery process involves validating hypotheses through interviews focused on pain severity, current workflow, and willingness to pay.
- [[DTCS Product Overview]] — DTCS connects various data types, including computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, and experimental data, to provide faster chemical understanding.
- [[Early Adopter Markets]] — Initial market focus should target early adopters in specific sectors like remote power, industrial process heat, and government-supported pilots.
- [[Financeable Energy Technology]] — For energy technologies to be considered financeable, investors require transparent, quantified metrics, and demonstrable proof of reliability through real-world data.
- [[Manufacturing Case Studies]] — This note analyzes how digital twin technology can bridge the gap between virtual simulation and physical manufacturing processes across industries like consumer electronics and semiconductors.
- [[New Product Development]] — Digital twins show potential value during New Product Development (NPD) because this phase involves high resource, labor, and trial-and-error costs.
- [[Portable Power Solutions]] — The technology is best suited for smaller-scale, portable, or remote power applications rather than immediate large-scale MW deployments.
- [[Target Customer Segments]] — The three primary customer groups identified are Big Tech, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors, each facing distinct pain points in chemical R&D.
- [[Third-Party Validation]] — Building trust in new technologies requires independent verification to prove reliability and lifespan.
- [[Virtual To Physical Connection]] — The core principle involves connecting virtual models with physical processes to achieve synchronized operation and mutual calibration.
