# Customer Discovery Interviews

Customer discovery interviews are designed to learn about a customer's current processes and pain points rather than attempting to sell a product. The process involves conducting multiple interviews across different segments to compare pain severity, current workflows, and willingness to pay. Key principles emphasize understanding the customer's existing challenges, such as slow characterization cycles or limited expertise.

## Key points
- The core principle during interviews is to understand the customer's current process first and not sell DTCS. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 4: Iteration Reflection*
- The interview focus is not on pitching the product, but on understanding the customer's most painful process issues. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 时间线与实践安排*
- The goal of the interviews is to determine if a digital twin tool can genuinely solve the identified problems. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 时间线与实践安排*
- The plan involves conducting six interviews across three segments to compare pain severity, workflow, and willingness to pay. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 5: Customer Discovery Plan — Next 6 Interviews*
- For a 20–30 minute interview, the notes recommend preparing about 8 core questions plus follow-ups instead of 17. — *[[General Alpha Interview Notes]], Finance Advisor — Finance 版 (¶51–62)*
- Each General Alpha question list (finance advisor, commercialization advisor, space startup founder) ends by asking who else the team should talk to. — *[[General Alpha Interview Notes]], Commercialization Advisor — Commercialization 版 (¶64–73)*
