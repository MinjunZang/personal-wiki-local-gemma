# Chemical R&D Pain Points

Hypothesized pain points in chemistry and materials R&D revolve around slow characterization processes, the high cost associated with lab time, and the need for stronger feedback loops between theory and experiments. These issues affect various groups, including established R&D teams, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors. The goal is to validate these pain points through customer interviews.

## Key points
- Long experiment or characterization cycles are identified as a pain point for Big Tech/Established R&D. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- Resource-constrained startups face issues like having too few specialists to interpret every dataset. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- A key pain point is the need for stronger theory-experiment feedback. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- These pain points are still hypotheses: as of the deck, no customer interviews had been done yet. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 4: Iteration Reflection*
- The potential outcome of the proposed solution is less trial-and-error and better use of characterization resources. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- The proposed solution aims to reduce experimental iteration, lab time, and use of scarce characterization resources. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 1: DTCS*
