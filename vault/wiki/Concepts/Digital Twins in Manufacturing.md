# Digital Twins in Manufacturing

Digital Twins aim to connect virtual models with physical manufacturing processes to solve the issue of siloed experimentation and simulation. This technology is particularly valuable for early prediction, in-process adjustment, and post-analysis by enabling virtual and physical systems to run synchronously and calibrate each other. The application is currently focused on chemical processes, offering benefits like reducing time and trial-and-error costs in manufacturing.

## Key points
- Digital Twins aim to connect virtual models with physical experiments or manufacturing processes to solve the problem of these elements being disconnected. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 核心规则与主题*
- The technology can be used for early prediction, for adjusting during experiments, and for later analysis. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 核心规则与主题*
- The current capability of the technology primarily focuses on chemical processes, such as semiconductor chemistry and surface treatment. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 互动与案例分析 > 讨论中的关键分歧与澄清*
- In the context of new product development, digital twins can help screen out optimal solutions early on when resources and trial-and-error costs are high. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 时间线与实践安排*
- A key value proposition is the ability to simulate multiple factors in a virtual environment to screen for better options before physical production, thereby saving time and reducing trial-and-error costs. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 互动与案例分析 > 典型案例*
- In semiconductor manufacturing, where physical experiments are costly and slow, digital twins can screen conditions quickly, but models must be calibrated with experimental data because accuracy requirements are very high. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 互动与案例分析 > 典型案例*
- The credibility of the system still relies on validation through real-world experiments, as virtual evaluations alone are insufficient. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 核心规则与主题*
