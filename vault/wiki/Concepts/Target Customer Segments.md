# Target Customer Segments

DTCS plans to interview three distinct customer segments to compare pain severity, workflow, and willingness to pay. These segments are Big Tech/Established R&D, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors. The interviews aim to understand current pain points rather than selling the product.

## Key points
- The three customer segments planned for interviews are Big Tech/Established R&D, resource-constrained startups, and raw-material vendors. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- The interview goal is to validate pain severity, workflow, and willingness to pay. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 2: Expanded Business Focus*
- Big Tech/Established R&D segments include materials and design engineers working with chemistry-heavy workflows. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- Resource-constrained startups are characterized as small teams with limited people, budget, and characterization access. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- Raw-material vendors include technical, application, or R&D teams that support material adoption. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- The six planned interviews intentionally span the three segments, two each, to compare them before narrowing the ideal customer profile. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 5: Customer Discovery Plan — Next 6 Interviews*
- The digital twin meeting chose the same three interviewee categories: engineers at large tech companies, startups or small and medium-sized companies doing chemistry or process development, and raw-material suppliers. — *[[Digital Twin Meeting Notes]], 时间线与实践安排*
