# DTCS

DTCS is a digital twin designed for chemical science that integrates computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, experimental data, and AI optimization. Its purpose is to help interpret spectra and infer reaction mechanisms, thereby speeding up chemical understanding. The product aims to reduce experimental iteration and the use of scarce characterization resources.

## Key points
- DTCS connects computational chemistry, spectroscopy simulations, experimental data, and AI optimization to help interpret spectra and infer reaction mechanisms. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 1: DTCS*
- The product's value proposition is to turn experimental spectra into faster chemical understanding. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- The team's starting hypothesis was that chemical characterization is slowed by weak real-time theory ↔ experiment feedback; no interviews had validated it yet. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 4: Iteration Reflection*
- The product can use AI optimization to guide the next experiment. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
- Potential customers include materials/design engineers, raw-material/application scientists, and scientists working with chemical reactions. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 1: DTCS*
- The product is hypothesized to benefit resource-constrained startups that have limited people, budget, and characterization access. — *[[DTCS Customer Discovery Deck]], Slide 3: Skinny Business Model Canvas — Initial Hypothesis*
